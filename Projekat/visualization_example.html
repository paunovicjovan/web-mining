Bought this phone about 3 months ago and overall I'm pretty happy with it. The camera is honestly amazing, takes super clear photos even in low light which I wasn't expecting at this price point. Battery easily lasts me a full day, sometimes more. My main complaints are the speaker — it's really tinny and quiet, not great for watching videos without headphones. Also the storage fills up fast, 32GB is just not enough anymore. Fingerprint sensor works great though, very fast. Build quality feels solid, no complaints there. Would probably buy again despite the speaker issue.
